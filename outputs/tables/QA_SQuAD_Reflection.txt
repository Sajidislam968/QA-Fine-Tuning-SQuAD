In this assignment, I learned how extractive question answering differs from text classification because the model predicts answer spans rather than assigning predefined class labels. After fine-tuning bert-base-uncased on SQuAD v1.1, the model achieved a validation Exact Match score of 80.17 and an F1 score of 87.75. Exact Match measures whether the normalized predicted answer matches a reference exactly, whereas F1 also gives credit for overlapping answer tokens. Tokenizing long contexts with overlapping windows and aligning answer character positions with token positions were important parts of preparing the dataset correctly. In the two custom tests, 2 prediction(s) matched the expected answer exactly as a string; the examples helped me inspect the model's ability to extract answers from new contexts. Overall, this project helped me understand how a pre-trained Transformer can be fine-tuned for extractive question answering and evaluated using EM, F1, and qualitative prediction checks.
